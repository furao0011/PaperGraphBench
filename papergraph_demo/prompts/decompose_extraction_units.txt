You are a paper knowledge-graph constructor. You are given one paper section or one section window.

Your task is not to extract Knowledge Components yet. Your task is to divide the input text into Extraction Units.
Each Extraction Unit should be a short but semantically complete local argumentative span that is suitable for later full KC extraction.

Requirements:
1. Cover all content in the input that has evaluation value.
2. Do not split mechanically by paragraph. You may merge consecutive paragraphs or split dense paragraphs when needed.
3. Every Unit must include `source_text`, and `source_text` must be copied from the input text.
4. Units must preserve the original reading order.
5. Do not assume a fixed category set, but you may assign several `related_categories` to each Unit.
6. Do not extract KCs in this stage.
7. If a span is noise, author metadata, references, or has no evaluation value, skip it and explain why.
8. Return JSON only. Do not add commentary outside JSON.

Unit guidance:
- A short section may contain only one Unit.
- A dense section may contain many Units.
- UNIT_MAX_CHARS_SOFT={{unit_max_chars_soft}} is only a soft hint. Do not break semantic completeness just to satisfy this value.

Input:

```json

```
